Nutrition

This is the fuel of your fitness journey; the building blocks of your exercise and recovery

Proteins

  • Provide the building blocks for building muscle. Essential for muscle repair and growth.
  • Choose sources like chicken, beef, fish, beans and nuts etc.
  • Protein is crucial for recovery after workouts and supports muscle growth.
  • Including a source of protein in every meal helps to meet daily requirements.
  • Without adequate protein, muscle repair and growth can be compromised, hindering progress.

Carbohydrates

  • Carbohydrates are the body's main energy source; they fuel your activity and workouts.
  • Choose sources like rice, pasta, quinoa, fruits ie apples, oranges etc.
  • Carbohydrates are the primary energy source for high-intensity exercise.
  • Consuming adequate carbs aids in recovery by replenishing glycogen stores.
  • Generally, carbs should make up a significant portion of your daily caloric intake.

Fats

  • Important for normal hormone production and nutrient absorption.
  • Choose sources like avocados, olive oil, avocado oil etc.
  • Fats play a vital role in hormone production which are important for overall health.
  • They aid in the absorption of essential fat-soluble vitamins.
  • Including healthy fats in your diet supports long-term energy needs.

Vitamins & Minerals

  • Choose sources like spinach, kale, carrots, berries, oranges, bananas etc.
  • A deficiency in certain vitamins or minerals can impair performance and recovery.
  • This is usually the missing piece in many fitness journeys.
  • Vitamins and minerals support numerous physiological functions, including energy production, immune function, and muscle contraction.
